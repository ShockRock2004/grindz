Grindz — train on purpose, know what you trained

Grindz is a training log. Record every set as you lift it, watch a muscle heat map fill in across your week, and see progression on the exercises you actually repeat. It is free, carries no advertising, and shows no ads or trackers.

It runs as an installable web app and as an Android app, sharing one account, so a workout logged on a phone at the gym is on the laptop by the time you get home.
